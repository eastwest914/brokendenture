Got Questions?

5 Frequently Asked Questions

1. What should I do if I need a Same Day Dental Appointment?

If you suffer a sudden dental emergency, contact our office immediately at 561-486-5061. We hold priority time slots explicitly for urgent cases and will accommodate you as an emergency patient today.

2. How is an Emergency Toothache Treatment handled during a visit?

Our practitioner performs a gentle exam accompanied by digital imaging to identify the underlying source of pain—such as deep decay, structural fractures, or pulp infection. We then immediately administer localized numbing relief and start appropriate curative care.

3. Can a Broken Tooth Repair save my original natural tooth?

Yes. Depending on how deeply the tooth is cracked, our qualified Emergency Dentist can repair it using dental bonding, porcelain crowns, or root canal therapy combined with a crown to fully restore strength and beauty.

4. What is the step-by-step procedure for Knocked Out Tooth Treatment?

Pick up the tooth solely by its upper crown (never touch the root), rinse it gently with water, and attempt to reinsert it in the socket if possible, or store it in milk. Reach our clinic within 30–60 minutes for the highest re-implantation success rate.

5. How do I know if my issue requires seeing an Emergency Dentist right away?

If you experience unbearable throbbing pain, unmanageable bleeding, noticeable facial swelling, a loose permanent tooth, or physical trauma, you require immediate care from an experienced Emergency Dentist.
